<h2>What's New in Kinopio</h2>

<p>This time around, the big new updates were guided by teams in the Kinopio Pilot Program. One team is now <a href="https://kinopio.club/blog/trove-brands">fully onboarded</a>, and the other one is still ongoing. The goal is to make Kinopio a tool you can enjoy using at home and at work.</p>


<aside class="badge info">
  <img src="https://updates.kinopio.club/pages/blog/posts/39/flowers.gif" alt="">
  If you'd like to try using Kinopio at work with your team, reply to this email. I'll onboard your team, upgrade their accounts, and provide priority support. Every couple weeks we'll talk about how it's going.
</aside>


<section class="subsection">
  <p>When you need to answer the questions “who’s doing what”, and “when is it due?”, <strong>@mention</strong> collaborators to assign or add a due date. If you're @mentioned, or the due date arrives, you'll get an email notification. Combine these with checkboxes and tags and you have all the elements of project management.</p>
  <img src="https://cdn.kinopio.club/Lg-MOqDVhi4kvz4xA_Py8/combined.webp" alt="@mentions, due dates, checkboxes and tags combined">
</section>


<section class="subsection">
  <p>Use the <strong>Right-Side TOC</strong> (Table of Contents) to jump between boxes and lists in a space. Also great for presentations because you can separate your ‘slides’ and use the TOC to display an overview of your talk and then jump to the next topic.</p>

<section class="subsection">
  <p>Sometimes you just want more control over connection lines. You may want them to come out from a different side, or to bend them around stuff. Well good news, now you can <strong>Drag to Bend Connections</strong>. It seems obvious in retrospect, but the design <a href="https://kinopio.club/blog/bending-connections">evolved over months</a>.</p>
  <video autoplay loop muted playsinline>
    <source src="https://updates.kinopio.club/pages/blog/posts/bending-connections/final.mp4">
  </video>
</section>


<section class="subsection">
  <p>Relatedly, you can now <strong>Connect to Lists</strong>, just like cards and boxes.</p>
  <img src="https://cdn.kinopio.club/4R_2LmBf4Fltmlo_RmaBI/CleanShot-2026-10-02-at-11.13.39-AM-2x.png" alt="Connecting to lists">

<section class="subsection">
  <p>A lot of people complained that liked the idea of <a href="https://kinopio.club/help/drawing">drawing</a> on their spaces, but you weren't able to <strong>Move Drawings</strong> with your content. Well of course, now you can.</p>
  <p>To move drawings, select them with either <code>cmd/ctrl-a</code> to select all, or by hovering over the window edge and dragging to <a href="https://kinopio.club/help/select-and-bulk-actions">select all below</a>.</p>
  <p>And if your drawing is fully inside a box, you can drag the box to move it. Kinda like a picture frame.</p>
