# Per Space Settings

TODO: why you would want to: different spaces have different purposes, so the way you work in them can be different too

Open Space Settings by clicking the `Space Settings button` in the footer.

Settings can apply to the `Current Space` only, or to `All Spaces`. Current Space settings override All Spaces settings.

<img src="https://updates.kinopio.club/pages/help/posts/space-settings/dialog.png" class="medium" alt="space settings dialog">


### New Card Color

Choose the default color for new cards that you create. Set it for the current space, or for all spaces. Click the `×` button to clear it and go back to the default.

### Disable Card Snapping

By default when you drag a card onto another card they snap together to form a list. But there are times when you might want that. 

> Sometimes I like to overlap images for a messy mood board aesthetic, but in other spaces I want things nice and neat and organized. 

The Disable Card Snapping button will set your preference to disable snapping in the current space. When snapping is disabled you'll also see an icon on the space settings button to remind you.
