# Drawing

Freehand some highlights, doodles, diagrams, and add a unique personal touch to your spaces. You can access the drawing tools through the left side toolbar. You can change color, stroke size, and toggle eraser mode.

# Moving Drawings

To move your drawings, select them by either `cmd/ctrl-a` to select all, or by hovering over the window edge to [select all below, or to the right](https://kinopio.club/help/select-and-bulk-actions). And if your drawing is fully inside a box, you can drag the box to move it.
