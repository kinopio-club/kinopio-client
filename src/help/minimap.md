# Minimap

Get a birds eye view perspective and jump around big spaces with the minimap. There are two ways to use the minimap, while panning and from the footer buttons.

## While Panning

The minimap will display while panning mode is active – when either the space-key or the right-mouse button is held down.

## Table of Contents

Hover over the visual Table Of Contents on the right side of the page for an easier and funner way to jump to your Boxes, Lists, and Line dividers. (The TOC is not available on mobile due to screen size restrictions).
