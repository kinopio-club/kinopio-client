# Minimap

Get a birds eye view perspective and jump around big spaces with the minimap. There are two ways to use the minimap, while panning and from the footer buttons.

## While Panning

The minimap will display while panning mode is active – when either the space-key or the right-mouse button is held down.

## Table of Contents

Hover over the visual Table Of Contents on the right side of the page for an easier and funner way to jump to your Boxes, Lists, and Line dividers. (The TOC is not available on mobile due to screen size restrictions).

### Hiding Items from the TOC

why you would want to: , for cleanliness, and to highlight main anchor points in a space

To hide a Box, List, or Line divider from the TOC, open the TOC dialog by clicking the TOC button below the right side Table of Contents (or with 'C'), then click the eye button next to the item.

Hidden items are faded out in the TOC dialog, click the eye button again to show them. Hidden items are saved with the space, so everyone who views it sees the same TOC.
